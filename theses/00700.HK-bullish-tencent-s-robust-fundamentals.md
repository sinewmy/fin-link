## Thesis

Bullish on Tencent's robust fundamentals, I firmly believe the company will spearhead a resurgence in the AI era

## Hypotheses

- **h1** [Core] Tencent will lead the AI era resurgence, driving a meaningful share of its revenue from AI products and services.
  - Observable: Tencent AI revenue as a percentage of total revenue (reported in quarterly earnings)
- **h2** [Sub (of h1)] Tencent’s robust fundamentals (strong operating cash flow) support continued investment in AI research and deployment.
  - Observable: Tencent operating cash flow growth YoY (quarterly)
- **h3** [Sub (of h1)] Tencent’s AI products (e.g., Hunyuan, Tencent Cloud AI) see increasing adoption among users and enterprises.
  - Observable: Monthly active users (MAU) for Hunyuan AI assistant and Tencent Cloud AI customer count
- **h4** [Sub (of h1)] Tencent maintains or improves its competitive position in the Chinese AI market versus peers like Alibaba and Baidu.
  - Observable: Market share in China’s AI-as-a-service market (from industry reports e.g., IDC)

## Invalidation conditions

- Tencent AI-related revenue declines year-over-year for two consecutive quarters
- Tencent’s operating cash flow growth turns negative year-over-year
- Tencent loses market share to Alibaba or Baidu in AI services (e.g., cloud AI revenue share drops by more than 5 percentage points)
- No meaningful AI product launches or updates for twelve months
